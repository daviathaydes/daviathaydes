## Hi there, I'm Davi Athaydes Leite 👋

I'm a Master's student in Political Science at the [Institute of Social and Political Studies (IESP-UERJ)](https://iesp.uerj.br/), with a interest in quantitative methods. Also, I hold a Bachelor's degree in Public Administration from [Universidade Estadual do Norte Fluminense (UENF)](https://uenf.br/portal/).

I am also affiliated with research groups such as the Center for Studies in Representation and Democracy (NERD) and the [Laboratory for Monitoring and Evaluation of Policies and Elections (MAPE)](https://mape.org.br/).

I am interested in elections and electoral politics, especially topics such as electoral competition, reelection, and legislative majorities. I also study the distribution of the federal government's discretionary resources and the political factors that influence it. Methodologically, I work with a quantitative approach, using observational data and causal inference.
